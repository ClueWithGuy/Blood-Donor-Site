One place for the university blood donation network

Drop For Life brings the important parts of the donation process into one system.

  • Donors can register and manage their availability.
  • Users can search donors by blood group.
  • Blood requests are stored in the backend database.
  • Donors can keep a history of previous donations.
  • Registered users can communicate through messaging.
